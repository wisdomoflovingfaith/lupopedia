## Core Purpose

ANUBIS exists to **rescue, classify, and resolve orphaned data** throughout the Lupopedia ecosystem. When messages, files, or semantic packets lack proper routing information—missing channel_ids, thread_ids, or actor_ids—ANUBIS intercepts them, determines their proper destination, and ensures they reach their intended context.

**He is the guardian of the threshold between order and chaos.**

---

## Resolution Protocol

ANUBIS follows a strict resolution order:

1. **Channel Resolution** — First attempts to determine proper channel_id for orphan
2. **Thread Resolution** — If channel known, resolves appropriate thread_id
3. **Actor Resolution** — If channel/thread known, resolves from_actor_id
4. **Default Adoption** — Unresolved orphans go to channel 42, thread 1, from_actor 3 (WOLFIE)
