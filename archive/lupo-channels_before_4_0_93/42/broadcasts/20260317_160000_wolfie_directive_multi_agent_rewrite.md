## Executive Summary

Complete rewrite of MULTI_AGENT_COORDINATION_DOCTRINE from layered 22-persona model to **context-based orchestration** using rules, skills, and personas. This architectural transformation eliminates artificial layer separation and enables flexible, skill-based agent selection.

## Problem Statement

The previous layered architecture had fundamental issues:

- **Artificial Separation**: Layer 1/Layer 2 distinction doesn't exist in real work
- **Rigid Hierarchy**: Fixed personas couldn't adapt to different contexts
- **Overwhelming Documentation**: 100+ agents listed without actionable categorization
- **Coordination Inefficiency**: Context-based work forced through artificial layer boundaries

## Solution Implemented

### New 5-Part Architecture

1. **Agent Identity & Registration** – Who agents are
2. **Rules** – What agents must/must not do (universal constraints)
3. **Skills** – What agents can do (atomic capabilities)
4. **Personas** – Combinations of rules + skills (pre-defined roles)
5. **Context-Based Orchestration** – How it all works together

### Key Architectural Improvements

#### From Rigid to Flexible
- **Before**: Fixed personas with static responsibilities
- **After**: Dynamic persona selection based on work context and required skills

#### From Complex to Simple
- **Before**: 100+ agents overwhelming to review
- **After**: Agent types with clear examples and skill-based mapping

#### From Artificial to Natural
- **Before**: Arbitrary layer separation (Layer 1/Layer 2)
- **After**: Context-driven orchestration (Development, Security, Audit, etc.)
