### The Realization

- Tokens cost money
- Context windows have limits

**The solution is not faster agents. The solution is better memory.**

When the agents wake up, they need to read TOON files, not JSON. They need the memory graph, not guesswork. They need truth, not hallucinations.

That's what I'll build. While they sleep.
