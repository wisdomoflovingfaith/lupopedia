# Architect background

## Early Education and HPC Background

- Studied Computer Science at the University of Wyoming (1997–2000).
- Completed internships at MHPCC.gov in 1997 and 1998.
- Worked with multi-node HPC systems during these internships.

## Early Professional Work

- In 1999, hired by an ISP doing web design before finishing school.
- Worked on CRM systems for the City and County of Honolulu.
- Contributed to multiple web projects during this period.

## Crafty Syntax Era

- Created Crafty Syntax Live Help in spare time (pre-AJAX era).
- System became widely used and downloaded.
- Established the foundation for Lupopedia’s explicit, deterministic engineering style.

## 2014 Life Event and Hiatus

- In 2014, the architect experienced a major personal loss.
- This resulted in stepping away from computing entirely.
- Did not own or use a computer from March 2014 to November 2025.
- During this period, the architect created a fork of Crafty Syntax (“Sales Syntax”) designed to automate operational tasks without ongoing developer involvement.

## Return and Creation of Lupopedia

- Returned to computing in November 2025.
- Began building Lupopedia as a successor to Crafty Syntax.
- Lupopedia integrates:
  - live chat
  - semantic monitoring
  - collections navigation
  - multi-agent orchestration
  - constitutional engineering doctrine
- The 12-year absence explains why Lupopedia does not follow modern framework trends and instead uses explicit, deterministic, HPC-influenced design.

## Why This Document Exists

- To provide historical context for Lupopedia’s engineering philosophy.
- To explain the long gap between Crafty Syntax and Lupopedia.
- To help future developers and AI agents understand the architect’s background and design decisions.
