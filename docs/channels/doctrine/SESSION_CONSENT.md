# Session Consent for Lupopedia

## 🐺 **How We Keep You Logged In**

### **For Anonymous Users**
We use standard session cookies to keep your basic session active. This ensures your chat sessions work properly while you browse.

### **For Logged-In Users**
With your consent, we use enhanced technical identifiers (cookies and limited fingerprinting) to keep you logged in across all Lupopedia properties. This includes:

- Session continuity across domains
- Enhanced security against account takeover
- Abuse prevention and platform protection
- Consistent experience across Lupopedia services

**Important**: We do NOT use this for advertising or tracking you across the wider internet. Our tracking is limited to keeping your Lupopedia session working properly and secure.

## 🛡️ **What This Means**

### **Anonymous Users**
- ✅ Basic session functionality
- ✅ Chat continuity during single session
- ✅ No cross-domain tracking
- ✅ No fingerprinting or enhanced identifiers

### **Logged-In Users (With Consent)**
- ✅ Enhanced session continuity across Lupopedia
- ✅ Improved security and abuse prevention
- ✅ Consistent experience across services
- ✅ No advertising or cross-internet tracking
- ✅ Data kept only as long as needed
- ✅ You can revoke consent anytime

By continuing to use Lupopedia, you agree to this limited, purpose-bound use of technical identifiers solely for session management and security within the Lupopedia ecosystem.

*This consent can be withdrawn at any time by logging out or managing your privacy settings.*
