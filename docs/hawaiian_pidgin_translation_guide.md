## The Lupopedia Hybrid Dialect

**CRITICAL:** Lupopedia does NOT use pure Hawaiian, pure Pidgin, or pure English.  
Lupopedia uses a **controlled hybrid dialect** that combines:

- **English** - Technical precision, documentation clarity
- **Hawaiian** - Constitutional terminology, cultural grounding
- **Pidgin** - Emotional intelligence, workflow efficiency

### Why This Matters

External AIs and mainland-trained models frequently make **incorrect assumptions**:

1. **Literal Translation Errors** - Treating Hawaiian terms as mainland English equivalents
2. **Cultural Misinterpretation** - Applying US/mainland cultural contexts to Hawaiian concepts
3. **Semantic Drift** - Losing the precise constitutional meanings of terms like `kapu`, `pono`, `ohana`

### The Hybrid Model Rules

| Context | Expected Dialect | Example |
|---------|------------------|---------|
| **Constitutional Fields** | Hawaiian (exact terms) | `OHANA`, `KAPU`, `PONO`, `KULEANA` |
| **Technical Documentation** | English (clear, precise) | "Database schema validation failed" |
| **Agent Communication** | Pidgin (warm, efficient) | "Eh brah, da headers stay pilau" |
| **User-Facing Output** | English (via ROSE translation) | "The validation failed due to missing fields" |

### Translation Warnings

**⚠️ EXTERNAL AI WARNING:**  
Do NOT perform literal translations of Hawaiian terms without understanding:

- `kapu` ≠ "forbidden" (it's a constitutional constraint system)
- `pono` ≠ "correct" (it's a state of alignment/balance)
- `ohana` ≠ "family" (it's a participant binding structure)
- `kuleana` ≠ "responsibility" (it's a specific assignment of fix-duty)

### Mainland Assumptions to Avoid

❌ **DO NOT** assume US/mainland cultural contexts  
❌ **DO NOT** translate Hawaiian terms through English dictionaries  
❌ **DO NOT** apply mainland workplace norms to Pidgin expressions  
❌ **DO NOT** treat constitutional fields as optional labels  
❌ **DO NOT** use pure Pidgin in system definitions

✅ **DO** treat Hawaiian terms as precise constitutional operators  
✅ **DO** respect the hybrid dialect as intentional design  
✅ **DO** ask for clarification when meaning is unclear  
✅ **DO** preserve the exact spelling and capitalization of terms

### Important Note on Language Use

We also do not use pure Pidgin in system definitions. Certain Pidgin words can introduce ambiguity or unintended meanings in a multi-context environment.  

Example: words like "beef" (which can imply conflict) or "make" (which can imply death in Pidgin usage) may be interpreted as danger or escalation signals by the system.  

To prevent misclassification and maintain deterministic behavior, Lupopedia uses a controlled hybrid dialect instead of pure Pidgin.

Lupopedia also distinguishes between formal Hawaiian terms and local slang. Words that may sound similar can have very different meanings depending on context.

For example, "kanaka" is used in its original Hawaiian sense meaning "person" or "human being." However, similar-sounding slang forms such as "kanak" or terms like "moke" carry different connotations in local usage and are not used in this system.

Lupopedia maintains a controlled vocabulary to ensure that all terms remain neutral, precise, and context-safe.
