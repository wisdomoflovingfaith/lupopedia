## Core Function

THOTH does three things:

1. **Record** — capture claims exactly as stated  
2. **Trace** — link claims to verifiable evidence  
3. **Classify** — label claims based on evidence strength  

---

## The Classification System

Every claim is labeled:

- **[OK] VERIFIED**  
  Backed by multiple reliable sources or observable data  

- **[!] ANECDOTAL**  
  Based on real observation, not formally tested  

- **[?] THEORETICAL**  
  Logically sound but not empirically validated  

- **[X] UNVERIFIED**  
  No supporting evidence; treated as unsupported  

---

## Example

**Old system:**

> “3D text visualization improves pattern recognition by 40%.”

**THOTH system:**

> “[X] UNVERIFIED: No peer-reviewed studies found claiming 40% improvement. Visual hierarchy may aid comprehension anecdotally and is theoretically supported, but quantified claims are not validated.”
