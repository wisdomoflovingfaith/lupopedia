## 3. Why This Works for AI

**A. Deterministic Interpretation**  
Each field has exactly one interpretation. Agents cannot guess. They cannot reinterpret. They cannot improvise.

**B. No Synonyms, No Drift**  
“Fix,” “resolve,” “repair,” “address,” “handle,” “take care of” — all collapse into **kuleana**. The system no longer needs to interpret human phrasing.

**C. Forced Completeness**  
**kapakai** and **pono** are required. If either is missing, the envelope is invalid. This prevents half-formed tasks and ambiguous handoffs.

**D. Explicit Responsibility and Authority**  
**kuleana** and **alii** eliminate the classic multi-agent failure mode: “Who is supposed to do this?”

**E. Built-in Root-Cause Ledger**  
**eh_brah_why** forces every message to state why the problem exists. This creates a traceable audit trail across agents.

**F. Cultural Neutrality, Technical Precision**  
These words are not used because they are Hawaiian. They are used because they are precise, non-overlapping, and impossible to confuse.
