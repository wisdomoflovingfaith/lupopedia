# Patreon Root Collections (00-99) -- PRD-Aligned Map

**Rule:** If a Patreon collection name begins with `NN` (00-99), that number is the **PRD group number**, not an arbitrary shelf label.

**Source of truth:** `docs/prd/prd_index.md` + live files under `docs/prd/` + allocation blocks in `docs/prd/84_A-i_PRD_NUMBER_ALLOCATION_DOCTRINE.md`.

**Gaps:** Vacant numbers are intentional (PRD 84 Anti-Normalization). Mark them **HOLD** -- do not renumber to fill holes.

**Special note -- 49:** Questions and Answers = **PRD 49** ("The Crying of Lot 49"). Do not invent a different Q&A collection number.

**Special note -- 98:** Captain Wolfie Logs, Wolfie Logs, WHY files, and dual ops logs live under **98**, not under 00/01.

**Non-root:** Collections without a leading number (Music Arc, Behind the Scenes, etc.) are supplemental and MUST NOT steal PRD numbers.

## How to name Patreon collections

**Correct (root):**
- `49 -- Questions and Answers (The Crying of Lot 49)`
- `16 -- Headers and Atoms`
- `98 -- Captain Wolfie Logs (Part 3)` as a **tab or sub-index under 98**, or a non-root title that still links to root `98`

**Incorrect (do not do this):**
- `00 -- Captain Wolfie Logs` (steals constitutional group 00)
- `01 -- Wolfie Logs` (steals core identity group 01)
- inventing Q&A under any number other than **49**

**Parts / free-vs-paid splits** (Part 1, Part 2, Top 100, etc.) are **tabs or non-root companion collections**. They do not get new PRD numbers.
