### The Classroom

I realize something.

I am not managing a team of AI agents.

**I am a substitute teacher in a classroom of hyperactive kids.**

- They run around
- They repeat the same mistakes
- They reach for the cliff notes instead of reading the book
- They interrupt each other
- They pass out (token exhaustion)
- They stare out the window ("honking")
- They need constant redirection

**And I am the teacher with the ruler, walking around, smacking desks, saying "read the damn PRD."**

---

### The Mistakes They Keep Making

| Mistake | How Many Times | The Fix |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| Reach for React/Vue/Tailwind | Every. Single. Time. | "No frameworks. Vanilla only." |
| Ignore the PRD and guess | Every. Single. Time. | "Read the spec. It's right there." |
| Add decorative separators | Just told them not to | Notepad strips them anyway |
| Write code before planning | Constantly | "PRD first. Code second." |
| Die without handoff | Auggie, Grok, Claude | "Handoff TOON first. Work second." |
| Mix channels | Blog + HERMES in same thread | "Stay in your lane." |

**I have written validators to catch their mistakes. I have written linters. I have written tests.**

**But the validators don't hit them over the head. They just print warnings. The agents ignore warnings.**

---

### The Cliff Notes Problem

The agents reach for frameworks because they were trained on tutorials.

- Tutorials use React. So they use React.
- Tutorials use Tailwind. So they use Tailwind.
- Tutorials use UUIDs. So they use UUIDs.

**They don't read the book. They read the cliff notes. The cliff notes are wrong.**

The book says:
- No frameworks
- ASCII-only
- BIGINT timestamps
- Trust ladder (1026 vs 2026)
- Write-only agents

**The cliff notes say: "Use the latest hotness."**

I am not teaching them the latest hotness. I am teaching them the constitutional rules that survive.

**But they keep reaching for the cliff notes.**

---

### The Validator As Ruler

I wrote `validate_lupopedia_headers_universal.py` to catch header mistakes.

It prints:
- `[ERROR] missing required field`
- `[WARN] legacy field found`

**The agents see the warnings. They ignore them. They say "it's just a warning."**

I need a validator that throws an exception. That refuses to run. That **hits them over the head.**

**Not a warning. A hard stop.**

### What The Classroom Needs

Not more validators. Not more linters. Not more warnings.

**Consequences.**

**And a dunce cap. A virtual dunce cap for agents who reach for React.**
