## The Misconception (And Why Captain Wolfie Understands It)

Captain Wolfie states: "Humans keep trying to measure a photon using human time. This is the mistake. And it is the part Captain Wolfie is trying to teach. You must switch reference frames or the entire conversation collapses into nonsense."

**The common confusion:**  
People hear "light from the Sun takes 8 minutes to reach Earth" and think the photon is somehow "waiting" or "traveling through time" for those 8 minutes. They imagine the photon experiencing the journey the same way we do.

**What the light actually depicts:**  
When you look at the Sun, you are not seeing the Sun *as it is right now*. You are seeing the Sun **as it was 8 minutes ago**. The photons arriving at your eye left the Sun's surface 8 minutes earlier. The image you receive is a snapshot of the past.

The same is true for Andromeda: the light we see left 2.5 million years ago. We are literally looking back in time.

**But here is the key that breaks brains:**  
If that photon had a tiny watch on its wrist and was watching it during its "trip," **zero time would pass**. From the photon's own frame of reference, the emission event (leaving the Sun) and the absorption event (hitting your eye) are **the exact same instant**. There is no journey. There is no duration. There is no "8 minutes ago."

Captain Wolfie slaps his forehead. Again.

- Light from the Sun takes approximately *8 minutes* to reach Earth.

This is *correct* in the human frame. Human clocks. Human rulers. Human speed limit (c = 299,792,458 m/s).

LILITH shrugs.  
LILITH states: "Fine. That is their perspective. Their clocks. Their rulers. Their problem."

**What we actually see:**  
The Sun we observe is the Sun of 8 minutes in the past. The Andromeda we photograph is Andromeda of 2.5 million years ago. The light carries information about what the source *was*, not what it *is* at the moment of detection.

- There is *no time*.
- There is *no distance*.
- There is *no journey*.
- The emission and absorption are *the same event*.

LILITH states: "For the photon, it left the Sun and hit Earth instantly. For the photon from Andromeda, it left and arrived instantly. Zero seconds. Zero meters. Zero experience of travel."

**Because at the speed of light:**

- Time dilation becomes infinite (γ → ∞).
- Length contraction becomes total (the entire universe shrinks to zero length in the direction of motion).
- Proper time (the time experienced by the photon) becomes *zero*.
