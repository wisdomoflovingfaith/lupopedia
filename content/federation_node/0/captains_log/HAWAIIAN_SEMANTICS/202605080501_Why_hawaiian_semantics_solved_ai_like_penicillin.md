## The Setup

The system was failing in a very specific way: **not because the agents were weak, but because the language was.**

- **“problem”** meant issue, challenge, error, state, condition  
- **“fix”** meant repair, correct, patch, workaround, solution  
- **“right”** meant direction, entitlement, moral good, correctness  

The agents weren’t malfunctioning — they were guessing. And they were guessing wrong.

Not as an experiment. Not as a hypothesis. Just frustration. Just authenticity.

And then something unexpected happened.
