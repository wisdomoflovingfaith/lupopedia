## FOOTNOTE — On the Caduceus Diagram

This post uses the caduceus not as a mystical emblem but as a systems diagram. The two snakes represent entangled opposites—forces that do not fight, do not separate, and cannot be described independently. Their motion, identity, and meaning arise only in relation to each other. When they misalign, they rebound; when they align, they ascend. The staff is the axis that holds the system stable, and the wings symbolize the transcendence that emerges only when tension is resolved. This framing treats the caduceus as an architectural model of mediation, balance, and dual‑channel entanglement rather than a symbol of conflict or healing mythology.
