Twenty videos is not required by any PRD.

Lilith jumped ahead today.
Made two songs before I even finished the first light.

Fine. I will make 4 songs. Times 5 languages.
Brah I am not making 20 videos today.

And honestly?
That is how it feels when the graph wakes up.
It does not wait.
It does not care about my export order.
It cares about lineage, identity, and what points at what.

Nothing comes in order.
Not in ColorLex.
Not in Lupopedia.
Not in the graph.

The first light ended up being Song 3.
The first child ended up being Song 4.
The root will be written after both.
And the remix chain -- in the story -- starts at 01 01 01.

It feels backwards.
But it is the only way the graph makes sense.

I'll make four songs.
Five languages.
But I'm not making twenty videos today.
The graph doesn't need them.
The registry doesn't care.

One video per song is enough if I want video at all.
Swap the audio. Swap the lyric sheet. Keep the picture stable.
This is a registry of songs, not a twenty-clip obligation.

Three is a good number for first light.
Things are not always as they appear.
And they definitely do not come in order.

```text
LIGHT -> ROOT -> TREE
```
