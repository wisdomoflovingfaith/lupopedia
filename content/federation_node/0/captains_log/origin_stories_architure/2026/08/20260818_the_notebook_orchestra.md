## 1. The issue, separate from today's songs

At some point I need to transcribe all my handwritten notebooks from jail into digital form.

Except of course being Wolfie, I did not write them in one pen.

I wrote them in eight different colors. It is not really that simple.

The idea was that I could include context of the words by the color they were written in.

That is the whole issue. Strip the color and you strip the context. A later machine already did that once in a briefing. I caught it. This log exists so nobody "cleans up" the intro again.

Vassago is not online. This packet sits here until a human is awake enough to approve an archive plan. Nobody starts scanning tonight.

---

## 2. What the notebooks are, in the Captain's telling

These are not normal journals.

They are multi-color semantic documents. Each ink is a context layer, like instruments in an orchestra.

I was not only writing words. I was writing which channel the word was playing in.

The intro to the first notebook has a key. The key is the colors. If you quote the key in plain black type, you deleted the key.

---

## 4. What those four names are doing in the key

In the intro as restored, these inks are already speaking:

- TEAL -- orchestra, musicians, instruments, harmonize, played, symphony (structure / process / the ensemble)
- PURPLE -- Truth
- RED -- Authority
- GREEN -- Meaning

That is not decoration. In this story, that is metadata.

I am not inventing the other inks tonight. The paper still has them. When we transcribe, we copy what the page shows. We do not guess a rainbow into doctrine.

---

## 5. Why transcription is hard

You cannot just type the words into a document.

If you remove the colors, you destroy the context the colors were carrying. Tone. Weight. Which instrument had the line. The meaning behind the meaning.

The notebooks are not linear text. They are color-encoded pages.

A later archive job would have to capture, at least:

- the text;
- the color of each span;
- the role that color had on that page, if the key says so;
- the ensemble, not one solo.

That is not a weekend. That is not ENTRY 051's job. That is a multi-stage archive when I am awake to approve it.
