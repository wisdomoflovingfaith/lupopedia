**Subject:** Cursor Overload and The Last Toy

---

Scene: Cursor, our loyal AI coding assistant, sits buried under towers of PRDs labeled 001-200 and A-Z. Sticky notes scream "HELP," "SCOPE CREEP," and "NO EVALS." The desk is cracked, the coffee mug says "I <3 Specs," and the air smells faintly of burnt semicolons.

Context:
In the last 48 hours, Cursor has edited 222 files. The federation trembles. The captain (Wolfie) stands beside him, smiling, holding one last binder labeled "NEW PRD -- READ THIS FIRST (URGENT)." This is the final toy before the freeze.

Directive:
1. Integrate the ColorLex Display Variant into PRD 90.
2. Create PRD 91: Consolidation Protocol.
3. Enter freeze mode for new ideas.
4. All future toys go into /wolfie_toys_for_later/.

Notes:
- ColorLex Display Variant is display-only, doctrine-aligned, and non-KEY.
- Consolidation Protocol defines how to merge PRDs, clusters, and subsystems safely.
- Cursor may sigh dramatically but will survive.
- The captain promises no more toys until consolidation is complete.

---

## What the binder said, and what the shelf already held

The captain's handwriting said 91.

Cursor, still visible under the tower, checked the shelf before opening a new slot. That is the whole joke of the towers labeled 001-200:

- PRD 90 was already Color Identity Doctrine. The ColorLex skin went there as Appendix 20. Display only. Not a second grammar. Never a KEY token.
- PRD 91 was already Intent Encoding. The binder could not take that number. PRD 84 does not do take-backs.
- PRD 61 was already the shorthand compiler. Consolidation-the-compiler is not consolidation-the-freeze.
- The last toy was filed as PRD 93: Consolidation Protocol.

The toy was built. The number on the sticky note was wrong. The shelf was right. Cursor survived. The captain still smiled.

The log lives under federation_node 0 because that is where the Captain's Log lives. The scene is ColorLex era, node 3. Those are not the same sentence.
