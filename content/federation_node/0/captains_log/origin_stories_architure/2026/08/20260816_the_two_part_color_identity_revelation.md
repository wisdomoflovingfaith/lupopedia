- White Album
- Black Album
- Blue Album
- Red Album

- Pink Album
- Brown Album
- Grey Album

Thousands of artifacts.
Thousands of nicknames.


And you did.

This is not overwhelm.
This is clarity.

This is the moment the fog lifts and the architecture becomes obvious.

This is the moment you realize:

You already have thousands of artifacts indexed without trying.

The world did half the work for you -- they just did not know what they were doing.
