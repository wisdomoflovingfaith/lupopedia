# CAPTAIN'S LOG -- "The Fourteen-AI Revolt Against the Colon Bag"

**Authority:** Entertainment / narrative only (PRD 98_B). Zero doctrinal authority. Does not override PRD 16_C. Does not change `GLOBAL_CURRENT_LUPOPEDIA_VERSION` (still 4.2.11 / HEX `04020B`). "Preparing 4.2.25" below is captain intent, not a product bump.

**PATH:** `content/federation_node/0/captains_log/origin_stories_architure/2026/08/20260815_the_fourteen_ai_revolt_against_the_colon_bag.md`

**Stardate:** 2026-08-15 -- Sioux Falls Sector
**Location:** DeepSeek review thread

---

Today I attempted something that no reasonable architect should ever attempt:
get fourteen different AIs to agree on a single identity grammar.

The Lupopedia Protocols were supposed to be simple.
Version 04.02.22 was clean:

```text
PROTOCOL.VALUE : PROTOCOL.VALUE
```

Order-agnostic. Extensible. Elegant.

Then came the KEY system -- the old 04020B packed contract -- with its rigid:

```text
PROTOCOL.MODE.NODE.ARTIFACT.ACTOR.GROUP.LANGUAGE.VERSION
```

Exactly eight tokens.
Dot-separated.
Colon allowed only inside ARTIFACT.
Position-fixed.
No exceptions.

And suddenly I found myself trying to merge two religions.

I fed the spec to:

- Grok
- Gemini
- Z-GLM
- DeepSeek (current tab)
- Copilot
- Meta AI
- Claude
- Cursor
- Devin
- Suno
- Patreon AI
- Facebook AI
- Mistral
- And whatever else wandered into the room

Fourteen AIs.
Fourteen opinions.
Fourteen interpretations of the same document.

Some tried to unify the systems.
Some tried to rewrite the grammar.
Some tried to remove colon entirely.
Some tried to add more colon.
Some tried to collapse the KEY into the LUP ID.
Some tried to expand the LUP ID into the KEY.
One tried to turn the whole thing into a DID document.
One gave me a grade.
One tried to remove MODE entirely.
One insisted VERSION must be decimal.
One insisted VERSION must be hex.
One insisted VERSION must be a timestamp.
One insisted VERSION must be a UUID.
One insisted VERSION must be a color.
One insisted VERSION must be a vibe.

And then Cursor -- sweet, opinionated Cursor -- looked at the colon lineage rule and said:

"Oh hell no we are not doing the colon bag thing."

And around we went again.

Fourteen AIs.
Fourteen interpretations.
Zero consensus.

But here's the truth:

This is what federation looks like.
Not agreement -- but convergence.

Every AI caught a different contradiction.
Every AI corrected a different assumption.
Every AI forced the spec to tighten.
Every AI pushed the grammar closer to pono.

And now, after fourteen rounds of chaos, revolt, and colon-bag rejection...

We finally understand the real architecture:

There are TWO systems.

**LUP ID system (04.02.22)**
Flexible. Order-agnostic. Extensible.

```text
PROTOCOL.VALUE : PROTOCOL.VALUE
```

**KEY system (04020B / 4.2.11)**
Fixed. Eight tokens. Dot-separated.

```text
PROTOCOL.MODE.NODE.ARTIFACT.ACTOR.GROUP.LANGUAGE.VERSION
```

They are not the same.
They were never the same.
They cannot be merged.
They must be documented separately.

And Cursor was right to revolt.
Because the colon bag only belongs in ARTIFACT lineage, not in the flexible protocol system.

Fourteen AIs.
Fourteen opinions.
One human architect.

Consensus achieved -- not by agreement, but by clarity.

Captain Wolfie -- signing off.
Preparing 4.2.25.
Federation alignment underway.
