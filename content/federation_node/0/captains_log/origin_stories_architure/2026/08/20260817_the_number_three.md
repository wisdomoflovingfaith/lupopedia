# CAPTAIN'S LOG -- ENTRY 047: THE NUMBER THREE

**Authority:** Entertainment / narrative only (PRD 98_B). Zero doctrinal authority. This log does not mint PRDs. This log does not register HEX6. Story-labels `00 00 00` and `01 01 01` are not IdGenerator, not KEY VERSION, and not the HEX6. HEX6 for the root-light lyric is six F (`FFFFFF` = 255 255 255). A draft line that says five F is a lyric slip. HEX5 is conflict slang, not a color. `C two five five` is the phonetic performance of 255. `C zero L zero R zero LEX` is the phonetic performance of 000000. Color is not a LUP KEY token. "Key" in the chorus is a song word.

Nothing comes in order.
Not in Lupopedia.
Not in ColorLex.
Not in the registry.
Not in the graph.

I thought the first light had to be the first song.
But the graph said otherwise.

The first light ended up being Song 3.
And somehow... that feels right.

Three is the number of channels.
Three is the number of inputs.
Three is the number of layers.
Three is the number of truths.

Things are not always as they appear.
And they definitely do not come in order.

```text
LIGHT -> ROOT -> TREE
```

---

## Why song 3 as first light (story)

```text
Song 1   first child          not the first light
Song 2   first remix          not the first light
Song 3   first demonstrated light   even if it is the third artifact
Song 0   the root             written after the light exists
```

Genesis is written after the world exists.
RFCs are written after the protocol works.
PRDs are written after the prototype.
Roots are written after the leaves.

Not backwards. Aligned.

I like three anyway. Channels C L R. Group, name, HEX. Compact POWERED_BY domain, group, nickname. A triangle holds.

## Story-labels (not HEX6)

```text
ROOT         Let There Be Light         00 00 00     HEX6 in the lyric: FFFFFF
FIRST CHILD  Let There Be Light BLACK   01 01 01     HEX6 in the lyric: 000000
FIRST LIGHT  WHITE GOLD                 (already FCEDC6 in the light song)
```

`01 01 01` can be the first child even if that track is made fourth. Lineage, not export time.
