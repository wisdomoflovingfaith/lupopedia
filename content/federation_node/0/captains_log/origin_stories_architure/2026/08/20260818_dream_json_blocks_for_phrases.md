## 1. I slept. I dreamed in JSON.

Ok. I got some sleep.

I was dreaming about JSON blocks. I have no notes this time, so I am just going off what I remember, which is not much. I want to park it in a Captain's log before it evaporates.

It would be nice if, when looking up a ColorLex code, you could pull a JSON block that tells you all the references, edges, and meta on that record.

In the dream it was not songs. It was phrases.

---

## 2. The mechanic, as I remember it

I typed something like:

```text
lookup("curiosity killed the cat")
```

I did not get a dictionary shrug. I got a JSON block.

Not the meme. Not the shallow first half. The edges.

References. Metadata. The hidden cultural links humans forget. The out-of-context damage.

The JSON was not "a vibe." It was context restoration.

That is the whole dream. Phrase in. Graph-shaped packet out. I am not assigning a ColorLex token to the phrase in this log. The dream had a code. I do not remember the numbers. I refuse to invent them now that I am awake.

---

## 3. Example from the dream: curiosity killed the cat

I pulled the JSON and got additional edges.

Cats have nine lives.

More importantly: curiosity killed the cat, but satisfaction brought it back.

That second half is the part people drop. Quote only the kill and you get a warning against asking. Keep the return and the proverb is not just "stop looking."

In the dream there was also an older edge: it used to be care killed the cat -- care as worry, not curiosity. I am not citing a library in this log. I am citing a dream. If we ever check it awake, we check it awake. We do not mint a row from a nap.

These edges matter because modern humans quote the first half and lose the rest.

---

## 4. Other out-of-context wrecks the dream waved at me

**My two cents**

Missing context, in the dream's telling: two cents from a poor person can weigh more than a million from a rich person, because it is more of what they have. I am not claiming that is the dictionary origin. I am claiming that is the edge the JSON showed me, and it is the kind of missing human context ColorLex-in-the-dream was trying to restore.

**Truth will set you free**

Missing context, in the dream's telling: not "truth" as a floating poster. A specific claim in a specific speech, and people lost which truth "he" was talking about. This log is not a church. It is a note that the quote travels without its room.

There are other phrases like that. Humans do this all day. First half. Meme. No edges.

That feeling is allowed. It is not a subsystem name. I am not christening Phrase Context Module, Semantic Edge Resolver, or Cultural Drift Corrector tonight. Those labels sound like product. This file is a nap.
