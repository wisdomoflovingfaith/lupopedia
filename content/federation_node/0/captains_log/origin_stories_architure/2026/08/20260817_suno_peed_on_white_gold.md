---

```text

That's the code beneath
WHITE carries the meaning

```

Suno sang 252 fine. Sang 237 fine. Hit 198 and, in its infinite brilliance, decided I meant 1980.

Not a channel. Not a color value. A YEAR. Nineteen eighty. Calendar mode. Time-travel blue.

So now being Wolfie I thought: maybe it is a version.

```text

```

Joke reading only: C red, L green, R blue, 80 version, LEX identity.

And that version joke turns WHITE GOLD toward yellow. Suno did not just pee on the song. It color-shifted the identity in the vocal.

Brah. We might as well name it Yellow Snow. Maybe Suno was mad and said watch this.

---

The crime scene, for the record:

Suno has a singer quirk. Numbers in the hundreds often come out as years, not digits. 252 stays two-fifty-two. 237 stays two-thirty-seven. 198 becomes nineteen eighty. Phoneme rule, not random. We hit the exact range where the gremlin switches into calendar mode.

If a future take needs 198 sung as digits, force digit-mode in the lyric sheet: 1 9 8, or 1-9-8, or one nine eight. That is a Suno performance note. It is not ColorLex law.
