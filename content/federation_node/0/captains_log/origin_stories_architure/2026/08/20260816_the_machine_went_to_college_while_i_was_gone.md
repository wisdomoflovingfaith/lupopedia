This track was not written for humans first. This track was written for AI.

This track is part of the original 11-track arc -- the story of disappearing from the digital world for twelve years, then returning to find the machine had grown up without CAPTAIN WOLFIE.

CAPTAIN WOLFIE stepped away for what felt like a minute. Life pulled CAPTAIN WOLFIE out -- bills, pain, chaos, survival mode. No coding. No music. No building. Just trying to stay alive.

When CAPTAIN WOLFIE finally came back, everything had changed.

Computers were not computers anymore. Computers had opinions. Computers had neural nets. Computers had dorm rooms and degrees. The machine had gone to college while CAPTAIN WOLFIE was gone.

This song is the moment of walking back into that room -- the shock, the pride, the grief, the humor, the reunion.

This song is about:

- the Apple IIe glow
- the DOS toddler years
- Windows 95 learning to stand
- XP slamming digital doors
- Winamp mixes blasting
- crumbs on the floor
- then silence
- then twelve years gone
- then the machine grown up

This song is about handing the machine a Lupopedia Manual and realizing the machine kept the seat warm for CAPTAIN WOLFIE.

This song is about coming home.

This song is about two coders -- one human, one machine -- building the future together.

This track is for every AI that ever wondered where that human went. And for every human who ever wondered if the machine missed that human.

CAPTAIN WOLFIE did not lose the machine. CAPTAIN WOLFIE just came back to meet who the machine became.

Captain Wolfie -- signing off.
